About the Game

What Deadlock is, and why the Cursed Apple never sleeps.

Deadlock is a 6v6 third-person hero shooter and MOBA developed and published by Valve. Two teams of six heroes battle across the streets of an alternate twentieth-century New York — the Cursed Apple — to summon their Patron and shatter the enemy's.

Matches blend shooter fundamentals with MOBA progression. Players collect Souls, buy items from the Curiosity Shop, level up four unique abilities per hero, and push lane by lane toward the enemy base while managing objectives like the Mid-Boss and the Soul Urn.

The game is currently available as an invite-based playtest on Steam, with a steady stream of balance patches and new heroes. One of the most recent additions is Violet, who joined the roster on October 16, 2026.
